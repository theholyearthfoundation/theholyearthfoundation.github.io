# 🧭 Roadmap

> The full, current version is a page:
> **[theholyearthfoundation.github.io/changelog-astro-public/roadmap](https://theholyearthfoundation.github.io/changelog-astro-public/roadmap)**.
> What's done is in the [changelog](https://theholyearthfoundation.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog).

The Holy Earth Foundation's GitHub home: Kenney's Teachings, an open record of the work on its sites,
and the way into both. This is where it's headed, in rough order. There are no dates: each step
happens when it's ready, and lands in the changelog first.

## Next

- **Kenney's welcome.** The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from Kenney, empty on purpose
  until he writes them.
- **A shared space for the community:** a WhatsApp group and letters on Substack. Both are marked
  "coming soon" in the Community window until they open.
- **A way to give to the foundation itself**, and the foundation's own words about gifts, so the
  placeholders on [`/donate/`](https://theholyearthfoundation.github.io/donate/) can go.
- **Give from a crypto wallet** on `/donate/`: one button for browser wallets, phone wallets and email
  sign-in. The slot is there now, empty on purpose.
- **A contacts page** (`CONTACTS.md`) for this organization, so the Reach out window's placeholder can go.

## Later, and still being explored

- **A network of our own.** A small social network owned by the community, on open protocols like
  Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO. Privacy first. A direction, not a promise.
